Tired of taking multiple screenshots just to cover a long, scrollable website?

Say goodbye to messy manual stitching and limited snipping tools. This extension gives you total control over what you capture. Whether you need a highly specific custom shape across multiple scrolls, a perfectly aligned bounding box, or a complete top-to-bottom page grab, you can do it all with pinpoint precision.

🔥 Core Features:

The 4-Peg Custom Capture: Drop 4 imaginary pegs anywhere on a webpage to create a custom capture zone. The shaded area shows exactly what will be included in your screenshot.

Drag & Readjust: Not perfectly happy with your initial placement? Simply click and drag any of your pegs to fine-tune the boundaries before you hit capture.

Instant Rectangle Mode: Need clean, standard edges? Click the "Make Rectangle" button to instantly snap your custom polygon into a strict, perfectly aligned rectangle, and easily readjust the bounds from there.

Full-Page Long Screenshots: Skip the pegs entirely when you need the big picture. Capture the entire scrollable length of a webpage in one seamless, high-quality image.

Perfect for:

Developers & Designers: Grabbing specific UI elements or layouts that span across the fold.

Researchers & Analysts: Clipping exact data tables or text segments without the surrounding webpage clutter.

Everyday Users: Anyone who wants total, frictionless freedom over their screen captures.
