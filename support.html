Before you write

A few useful checks.

Capture did not start?

Reload the tab after updating PegShot, then use the toolbar button or Alt + Shift + P.

License question?

Use the email address you want us to reply to, but never paste your Gumroad license key into a support message.

Sharing a bug?

A short description, page URL, and browser/extension version are more useful than a sensitive screenshot.
